# Not legal advice

StudyAbroad Law Assistant explains public official texts in plain language. It is not a lawyer, not the immigration authority, and not your university.

- An answer can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date even when it cites a source.
- A permit, scholarship, or university decision is made by the official body, not by this app.
- Before you apply, travel, work, or pay a fee, read the cited page and check it with the National Directorate-General for Aliens Policing (OIF), Tempus Public Foundation, or your university.
- Every answer must show at least one source link and that source's last-updated date. If it cannot cite a locked official source, it must refuse and point you to the official office.
- Do not send passport numbers, residence-permit numbers, or other personal data to the app.

The corpus is limited to the public URLs in `configs/sources.yaml`. Those pages were locked on 1 November 2026. A later change on a website is not in the app until that page is fetched again.
